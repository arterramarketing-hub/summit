# Cleaned sounds: report

65 clips from commit 7890e7e (`Assets/Summit/Resources/Audio/`), cleaned into this folder with the same names. Nothing in the project was changed. Scripts: `../cleantools/` (`plan.py` holds every cut, `render.py` does the work, `verify.py`/`wrapcheck.py` the checks).

## How

- Format: 44.1 kHz, 16-bit (triangular dither). Music is stereo; everything else is mono, because `SummitImport` imports every non-music clip with Force To Mono anyway.
- Every clip: DC removed and a 12 dB/oct high-pass (25-30 Hz for beds, 40 Hz for impacts, 70-80 Hz for voices, 100-300 Hz where the file was mostly wind rumble on the mic or a bird).
- Loops: the steadiest stretch was found by scanning 25 ms windows in three bands (full, <180 Hz, >2.5 kHz) for peaks/energy jumping 6-10 dB over the running median; stray events inside a kept stretch were spliced out with short equal-power crossfades; the tail is crossfaded (equal power, sample-exact) into the head. Every wrap was checked: the sample jump at the wrap is within normal sample-to-sample steps, and the level across the wrap stays inside the loop's own range.
- One-shots: the best single take; start 0-6 ms before the onset (detected against the background noise), end where the decay falls 45 dB under the peak or into the noise floor, with short fades.
- Levels: loops matched to the old clip's integrated loudness (measured on the loop played 3 times); one-shots matched to the old clip's maximum momentary (400 ms) loudness, measured with 1 s of padding. True peaks are held at -1 dBTP with a 4x-oversampled limiter, allowed at most 6 dB of limiting (3 dB for music and the UI click). Where a real, spiky recording could not reach the old (dense, synthetic) clip's loudness inside that, it is left at the ceiling, i.e. peak-matched to the old clip, and the shortfall is listed.

## Notes for the project

- **Wind levels in code:** commit b05f7d1 set `SoundMix.WindLow = 0.08` and `WindHigh = 0.26` for the recordings now in the project (its comment: wind_low -19.6 dBFS RMS, wind_high -18.2). The cleaned files are wind_low -25.1 dBFS RMS (5.5 dB quieter) and wind_high -14.7 (3.5 dB louder). With those constants unchanged, wind_low lands about 5.5 dB under the comment's -42 dBFS target and storm gusts about 3.5 dB over its -30. To keep the comment's targets with these files: WindLow ~0.15, WindHigh ~0.17; or leave WindLow for an even quieter bed.
- **Stereo files now in the project:** if Unity's Force To Mono averages the channels (and does not normalize), the current stereo clips (wind_high, ambience, carve, skid and others) play about 3-6 dB under their measured stereo loudness. These mono files are matched to the old mix, so some (wind_high, carve, skid) will sound louder than the current versions, as the old mix intended.
- **Unity Normalize:** the clips' `.meta` files have `forceToMono: 1` and `normalize: 1`. If Unity peak-normalizes clips on import, it undoes file-level gain (wind_low's -9 dB and the quieter ambience among them). Worth checking in the Inspector, or turning Normalize off in `SummitImport`.
- **Played as loops by the game but listed as one-shots:** `boost` (`Loop("boost")`) and `bear_growl` (3D source with `loop = true`). boost is made a seamless loop; bear_growl is a trimmed growl that wraps without a click.
